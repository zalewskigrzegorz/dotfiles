# Forms on Greg's Behalf: Subjective Fields Are His

A Performance Review self-assessment was submitted with guessed 1–5 ratings ("Initiative 5, bo napisał pitch") and a 1:1's mentoring direction backwards ("LOL JA jestem jego mentorem").

1. **Split the fields first.** Factual ones have a source (PRs, dates, names, ticket numbers); subjective ones don't (ratings, scales, checkboxes, "strengths", who mentors or reports to whom).
2. **Fill the factual fields; never invent the subjective ones.** Ask Greg (one `AskUserQuestion` per field group) or leave them empty and say so in one line.
3. **A value you propose is labelled `propozycja`** in the chat before Submit. "Done" after a human step is not consent to guessed values: if the snapshot shows nothing changed, say so and ask again before Submit.
4. **A relationship direction comes from the content**, not the title ("X:Greg" says nothing about who mentors whom).

Applies to `browsentic`, `vimma`, `agent-browser` and any API that submits a form.
