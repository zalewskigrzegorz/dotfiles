# Money figures: label the tax status and the source

A 3 968 zł instalment from a dealer's PDF stood next to "ok. 3 120 zł" from the configurator with nothing telling them apart ("czy to potwierdzone info i mówimy o cenie brutto?").

- Every amount in prose, a table or a popup says **brutto** or **netto**, and its source: `oferta` (document or mail), `konfigurator` (vendor's tool), `szacunek` (our arithmetic).
- A recommendation never rests on a `szacunek` without saying so.
- Currency conversions state the rate (`przy 3,67`). Amounts for Greg are in zł first, the foreign one in parentheses: "~37 zł (7,70 CHF)" (db368cfb).
- A price of goods is the final sum (goods + shipping + VAT / duty); unknown shipping → "bez wysyłki, nie sprawdzone" in the same line. Shops are compared by the final sum ("$39" came without €20 shipping).
